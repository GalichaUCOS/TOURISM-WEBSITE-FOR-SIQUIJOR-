Welcome to Suroy

Suroy is a travel agency committed to promoting Siquijor’s local tourism while supporting community growth and environmental sustainability. By showcasing the island’s natural beauty, rich culture, and unique charm, we aim to provide meaningful travel experiences that contribute to both the local economy and the preservation of its ecosystem. With a deep passion for responsible tourism, we strive to create unforgettable journeys while ensuring that Siquijor remains a thriving destination for generations to come.
